You are invited to Diktyo K's event on Saturday, September 19th at 19:00, at the Koufalia Cultural Center "K. Chatzakos", on the topic: "Mental & Physical Health".

Scientific findings support the view that exercise is an important factor in physical health. More recent research, however, highlights its equally regulatory role in mental and cognitive health, as well as in slowing cognitive aging.

At the event "Physical and mental health: exercise as a substitute for a lost way of life", our speakers, Mr. Michalis Minoudis and Mr. Charalambos Tsorbatzoudis, together with Ms. Maria Christoforou, will discuss the relationship between exercise and mental and cognitive health. Drawing on their scientific expertise, they will also highlight the importance of exercise as a key factor for a better quality of life.
